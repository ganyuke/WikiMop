WikiMop is for administrators of Fandom wikis who want to keep watch from their phone.

Add a wiki by its address, then sign in with a Fandom account or a bot password, or use it read-only with no account. Several wikis can be added.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Recent changes and your watchlist, with unpatrolled edits marked in red</li>
<li>Diffs with patrol, rollback, undo, block, and delete one tap away</li>
<li>Search, page history, protect, and move</li>
<li>New pages, blocked users, logs, and a user lookup</li>
<li>Editor with syntax coloring, completion of page and template names, find and replace, and preview</li>
<li>Drafts saved on the phone as you type</li>
<li>A confirm sheet before every change that lists what will be done</li>
<li>Read-only mode and required edit summaries in Settings</li>
<li>A daily edit streak card</li>
</ul>
